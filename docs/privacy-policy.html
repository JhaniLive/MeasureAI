MeasureAR Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

In short: MeasureAR uses your camera only to measure things in front of you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your measurements and photos stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

Camera

MeasureAR needs the camera to see surfaces and track the phone's position, which is how augmented-reality measuring works. The camera feed is processed on the device in real time. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, except for the screenshots described below.

What is stored on your phone

  • Measurement history: values you measure (lengths, areas, volumes, angles) and the time you took them.
  • Screenshots: a picture of the screen when a measurement is saved or when you tap the camera button.

These are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can delete them at any time in History, and they are removed if you uninstall the app.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your phone unless you tap Share or Copy. Then the measurement text or screenshot goes only to the app or person you pick, through Android's standard share sheet.

No data collection

MeasureAR does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not request internet access.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

MeasureAR is a measuring tool for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
