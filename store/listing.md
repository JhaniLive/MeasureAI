## Full description (max 4000)
MeasureAR turns your phone's camera into a tape measure. Point at a floor, table or wall,
tap two points and get the distance instantly, drawn right on top of the real world.

NINE WAYS TO MEASURE
• Line: the distance between any two points
• Height: mark the base and tilt up to the top. Great for furniture, doors and people
• Distance: how far away something is from you
• Angle: the angle at any corner
• Path: add up a route of many segments
• Rectangle: tap three corners for width, length, area and perimeter
• Circle: diameter, circumference and area
• Area: trace a room's floor corner by corner for its area and perimeter
• Volume: a box's base plus its height for W × D × H and volume

BUILT FOR PRECISION
• Detected surfaces are shown as a teal grid. Points placed there are the most accurate
• A magnifier appears when you hold steady, so you can place points exactly
• Drag any point to fine-tune it, and every measurement updates live
• Lines snap vertical, level, parallel and perpendicular
• Approximate readings are always marked with ≈, so you know when to trust a number

EASY TO USE
• Tap anywhere or use the Stamp button to place points
• Metric (cm / m) and imperial (ft / in) units
• Every measurement is saved to History with a photo. Copy or share it in one tap
• Built-in bubble level

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet access at all. Your measurements and photos
stay on your phone.

TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
• Good light and surfaces with some texture help your phone detect them quickly
• Move the phone slowly at first so it can understand the room
• For long distances, walk closer to each point
AR measurements are estimates. For critical work, confirm with a physical tape measure.

Requires a phone that supports Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).
