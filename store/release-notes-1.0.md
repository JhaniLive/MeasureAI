## MeasureAR 1.0

Measure anything with your phone's camera. Free, private, no account.

**Download:** `MeasureAR.apk` below, or visit https://jhanilive.github.io/MeasureAI/

### What's inside
- **Nine measuring modes:** Line, Height, Distance, Angle, Path, Rectangle, Circle, Area, Volume
- **Detected surfaces** shown as a teal grid; points placed there are the most accurate
- **Tap anywhere** or use Stamp to place points; **drag** any point to fine-tune it
- **Magnifier** for precise aiming, following your finger while you drag
- Snaps to vertical, level, parallel and perpendicular
- Approximate readings are always marked **≈**
- **History** with photos; copy or share any measurement
- Metric and imperial units, plus a built-in bubble level

### Install
1. Download `MeasureAR.apk` on your Android phone and open it.
2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
3. Open MeasureAR and allow the camera. Install *Google Play Services for AR* if prompted.

Requires an ARCore-supported phone running Android 8.0+.

### Privacy
No ads, no tracking, no internet access. Measurements stay on your phone.
