## MeasureAR 1.1

**Download:** `MeasureAR.apk` below, or visit https://jhanilive.github.io/MeasureAI/
Installs over 1.0 — your saved measurements are kept.

### New
- **🏢 Far height:** measure buildings, trees and poles tens of metres away. Aim at the base, then the top: the app uses the ground under you and the phone's angles, and shows an honest ± for the distance.
- **🎯 Calibrate with a card:** measure any bank or ID card once (mode picker, then *Calibrate with a card*) and every length, area and volume is corrected for the session.
- **🧭 Compass:** a new tab next to Level, with a rotating dial, heading and direction, and *Lock bearing* to hold a direction.
- **📶 Live accuracy meter:** "± 1.8 cm" with signal bars under the target distance, so you can see how precise the next point will be before you stamp.
- **📏 Tape-measure fractions:** imperial lengths read like a tape (5 ⅜″, 3′ 4 ½″).
- **⚡ Shortcuts:** long-press the app icon for *Height*, *Room area* or *Building height*.

### Improved
- Heights: tap the base, then tap the top
- Heights no longer grab the table *behind* an object, which made them read short
- The result card names the finished value
