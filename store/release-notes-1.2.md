## MeasureAR 1.2

**Download:** `MeasureAR.apk` below, or visit https://jhanilive.github.io/MeasureAI/
Installs over 1.1 — your saved measurements are kept.

### New
- **🖼 Hang pictures:** set how many frames, their size and the gap, tap the wall — the frames are laid out perfectly level with a ✕ at every nail.
- **🛋 Will it fit?:** pick a sofa, bed, fridge… (or type a size) and place a life-size box on the floor; drag and turn it to see if it fits.
- **🎨 Materials:** after measuring an area, work out paint (coats, doors, windows, can size), tiles and flooring.
- **🗺 Floor plans:** turn a measured room into a clean plan image with wall lengths and a scale bar — save or share it.
- **📁 Projects:** group saved measurements in History and export a PDF report.
- **❓ Step-by-step help:** a ? on every screen opens an illustrated guide; each guide also opens once the first time.
- **🌐 Languages:** हिन्दी, اردو and తెలుగు, with a language picker in the mode sheet.
- **👋 Welcome:** a short introduction on first launch, before the camera permission.

### Improved
- A calmer screen: one instruction line above STAMP, a slimmer top bar with a ⋯ menu, and more camera view
- Accuracy in words — Good, OK, Rough or Estimate (tap for the exact ±)
- Modes grouped (Measure, Room & floor, Home, Outdoors) with your recent ones on top
- A short vibration when a point is placed; whole numbers read "90 cm", small areas in m²
- Tape ticks along lines; floor plans drawn true to size

### Fixed
- Changing the language could crash the app
- Switching from Urdu back to another language kept the right-to-left layout
